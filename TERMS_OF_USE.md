# PathTrove Research Use Terms v1.0

**Effective date: 2026-08-28**

[中文版本](#pathtrove-科研使用条款-v10)

These terms govern the use of PathTrove's curated resources. Public access does not place those resources in the public domain and does not make them open-source or open-data materials.

## 1. Covered PathTrove resources

These terms apply to PathTrove-curated content, including:

- the PathTrove data table and its structured records;
- dataset reports and paper reports;
- PathTrove-authored explanations, normalized fields, provenance records, and other curated artifacts; and
- examples or exports that state that these terms apply.

These terms do not claim ownership of underlying third-party datasets, papers, independently established facts, or information obtained independently from primary sources. Those materials remain governed by their respective rights and terms.

## 2. Permitted research use

Without requesting additional permission, users may:

- view, search, and query PathTrove for non-commercial research, education, and personal study;
- make limited local copies reasonably necessary for such use;
- link to the original PathTrove table, report, or repository;
- include limited quotations, screenshots, or individual field examples in scholarly papers, theses, presentations, reviews, and teaching materials, with clear attribution; and
- publish analyses, statistics, or research results that do not reproduce or allow reconstruction of all or a substantial part of a PathTrove resource.

Use must remain consistent with the access conditions and rights of the underlying third-party resources.

## 3. Commercial use requires permission

Prior written permission is required for any use primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation, regardless of the user's institutional type.

This includes, without limitation:

- resale, licensing, or paid access;
- use in a paid product, service, database, API, report, or consultancy deliverable;
- use as a substantial component of a commercial search, recommendation, RAG, or AI system; and
- internal development that directly supports a commercial product or service.

Commercial and partnership permissions may be requested from the PathTrove maintainers.

## 4. No unauthorized redistribution

Without prior written permission, users must not reproduce, mirror, scrape, systematically extract, package, upload, publish, sublicense, sell, or otherwise make available to any third party all or a substantial part of a PathTrove resource.

In particular, users must not:

- republish the PathTrove table or a substantial subset of its records;
- mirror, bundle, or redistribute dataset reports or paper reports;
- create or distribute a substitute database, report collection, corpus, or service based substantially on PathTrove content;
- distribute such content through an open-source or closed-source repository, dataset hub, website, API, model package, or commercial product; or
- remove rights notices or circumvent technical access restrictions.

This restriction applies whether redistribution is free or paid, public or private, and whether the content is unchanged or modified. It does not prohibit the limited scholarly quotation and non-reconstructive research outputs permitted in Section 2.

## 5. Attribution and scientific citation

Clear attribution is required whenever permitted quotations, screenshots, or field examples are reproduced.

Researchers whose dataset discovery, selection, analysis, or study design materially benefits from PathTrove are strongly encouraged to cite PathTrove correctly and include the access date for the continuously updated table or reports:

> PathTrove Contributors. *PathTrove: Toward Ready-to-Use Clinical Data Services for Computational Pathology*. https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD.

Citation metadata are available in [`CITATION.cff`](./CITATION.cff).

## 6. Third-party rights and access conditions

PathTrove does not host, distribute, or sublicense the underlying pathology data. Inclusion of a resource does not grant access or reuse rights and does not supersede the original provider's license, terms of use, data-use agreement, ethical requirements, privacy obligations, or access restrictions.

License values recorded in PathTrove reports describe the relevant upstream resource. They do not automatically license the PathTrove report or other curated PathTrove content under the same terms.

## 7. Accuracy and responsibility

PathTrove is provided for research and informational purposes. Its records and reports may contain errors, omissions, outdated information, unresolved conflicts, or interpretations that differ from those of the original providers. Users must verify decision-critical information against the cited primary sources.

PathTrove is not medical advice, regulatory guidance, or confirmation that a resource is suitable for a particular study. Users remain responsible for scientific validation, legal and ethical compliance, privacy protection, and required approvals.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, PathTrove resources are provided as-is, without warranties regarding accuracy, completeness, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement.

## 8. Permissions and corrections

To request commercial use, redistribution, bulk access, or another use outside these terms, contact the PathTrove maintainers through the [PathTrove GitHub repository](https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove).

Factual corrections can be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove/issues) with the affected resource, field, proposed correction, primary source, and access date.

# PathTrove 科研使用条款 v1.0

**生效日期：2026-08-28**

本条款适用于 PathTrove 整理资源的使用。资源可以公开访问，不代表其进入公有领域，也不代表其属于开源或开放数据材料。

## 1. 适用的 PathTrove 资源

本条款适用于 PathTrove 整理的内容，包括：

- PathTrove 数据表格及其中的结构化记录；
- 数据集报告和论文报告；
- PathTrove 原创的解释、规范化字段、来源追溯记录及其他整理产物；
- 明确声明适用本条款的样例或导出内容。

本条款不主张拥有第三方底层数据集、论文、用户独立确认的事实，或用户独立从一手来源取得的信息。此类材料仍受其各自权利和条款约束。

## 2. 允许的科研使用

无需另行申请，用户可以：

- 为非商业科研、教育和个人学习浏览、检索和查询 PathTrove；
- 制作上述用途合理所需的有限本地副本；
- 链接 PathTrove 原始表格、报告或仓库；
- 在明确注明来源的前提下，于论文、学位论文、演示、综述和教学材料中有限引用文字、截图或个别字段示例；
- 发表不会复制或使他人能够重建 PathTrove 全部或实质部分内容的分析、统计或研究结果。

上述使用仍须遵守第三方底层资源的访问条件与权利要求。

## 3. 商业使用须取得许可

凡主要目的为获取商业利益或金钱报酬的使用，无论使用者属于何种机构，均须事先取得书面许可。

包括但不限于：

- 转售、许可或收费访问；
- 用于付费产品、服务、数据库、API、报告或咨询交付物；
- 作为商业检索、推荐、RAG 或 AI 系统的重要组成部分；
- 直接支持商业产品或服务的内部研发。

商业使用和合作授权可以向 PathTrove 维护者申请。

## 4. 禁止未经许可的二次分发

未经事先书面许可，用户不得复制、镜像、抓取、系统性提取、打包、上传、发布、转授权、出售，或以其他方式向任何第三方提供 PathTrove 资源的全部或实质部分。

用户尤其不得：

- 重新发布 PathTrove 表格或其中的实质性记录子集；
- 镜像、打包或分发数据集报告或论文报告；
- 以 PathTrove 内容为实质基础创建或分发替代性数据库、报告集合、语料库或服务；
- 通过开源或闭源仓库、数据集平台、网站、API、模型包或商业产品分发上述内容；
- 删除权利声明或绕过技术访问限制。

无论二次分发免费或收费、公开或私下进行、保持原样或经过修改，上述限制均适用。但本条款不禁止第 2 节允许的有限学术引用及不可重建原资源的研究产出。

## 5. 署名与科研引用

凡在许可范围内引用文字、截图或字段示例，均须明确注明来源。

如果 PathTrove 对研究者的数据发现、数据选择、分析或研究设计提供了实质帮助，我们强烈建议研究者正确引用 PathTrove，并为持续更新的表格或报告注明访问日期：

> PathTrove Contributors. *PathTrove: Toward Ready-to-Use Clinical Data Services for Computational Pathology*. https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD.

机器可读引用信息见 [`CITATION.cff`](./CITATION.cff)。

## 6. 第三方权利与访问条件

PathTrove 不托管、分发或转授权底层病理数据。收录某项资源不代表用户因此获得访问或复用权，也不会取代原始提供方的许可证、使用条款、数据使用协议、伦理要求、隐私义务或访问限制。

PathTrove 报告中记录的许可证描述相关上游资源，不代表相同许可自动适用于 PathTrove 报告或其他 PathTrove 整理内容。

## 7. 准确性与责任边界

PathTrove 仅用于科研和信息参考，其记录和报告可能包含错误、遗漏、过时信息、尚未解决的来源冲突，或与原始提供方解释不同的内容。用户必须回到所引用的一手来源核验影响研究决策的重要信息。

PathTrove 不构成医疗建议、监管指导，也不确认某项资源适合特定研究。用户仍须自行承担科学验证、法律与伦理合规、隐私保护和获得必要审批的责任。

在适用法律允许的范围内，PathTrove 资源按现状提供，不对准确性、完整性、特定用途适用性或不侵权作保证。

## 8. 许可申请与纠错

如需商业使用、二次分发、批量访问或本条款范围外的其他使用，请通过 [PathTrove GitHub 仓库](https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove) 联系维护者。

事实性错误可以通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DearCaat/PathTrove/issues) 提交，并注明涉及的资源、字段、建议修正内容、一手来源及访问日期。
